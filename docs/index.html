Keepreel

Host your own videos. Keep every embed.

Keepreel moves a Vimeo library into a storage bucket you own, then hands you a new embed code for every video. It is a one-time purchase for people whose hosting bill jumped: wedding and corporate videographers, small studios, schools and photographers.

Get it from Microsoft Get it on Google Play Website

How it works

  1. Connect your own Vimeo access token and a Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2 or Bunny Storage bucket.
  2. Keepreel downloads each video, makes it stream-ready on your device (HLS, no re-encoding, no quality loss) and uploads it to your bucket.
  3. Copy the new embed codes, or export a CSV of old and new codes for find-and-replace on your sites.

Pricing

Windows: your first 5 videos free; Keepreel Pro is $99 once for the whole library, on 2 computers. Android: free with ads, with a short ad for every 5 more videos.

Good to know

  • No Keepreel server: files go from Vimeo to your device to your bucket.
  • Links are unguessable but not password-protected.
  • No view counts, comments or client review tools.
  • Downloading needs a paid Vimeo plan, so move before your renewal date.
